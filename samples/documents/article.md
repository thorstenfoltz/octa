## Why the shot matters

A double shot is about 18 g of coffee in and 36 g of liquid out, in roughly
25 to 30 seconds. Everything after that is preference.

| Drink       | Shots | Milk |
|-------------|-------|------|
| Espresso    | 1     | no   |
| Flat white  | 2     | yes  |
| Latte       | 1     | yes  |

- Grind finer if the shot runs fast.
- Grind coarser if it chokes.
- Weigh the dose; guessing is where consistency goes.
